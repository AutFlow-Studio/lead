What is Anything.ai?

Anything.ai is a frontier AI company focused on autonomous agents that operate in the real world — watching signals, making decisions, and triggering actions across your workflows.

Our Work Spans

  • Upgrading digital strategy for major political campaigns
  • Helping a leading retail bank understand the true health of its loan book
  • Modelling COVID-19 growth vectors and cluster risks for public-sector planning
  • Building next-generation autonomous finance and research agents

Where trust and incentives matter, our agents can connect to a verifiable trust layer so behaviour, governance, and value flows are transparent and auditable — without turning the story into “just another crypto project.”

Where our agents work today

We focus on high-impact, high-stakes problems — where better decisions and intelligent automation truly change outcomes.

Banking & Financial Services

Our agents monitor loan books, customer segments, and product performance in real time. They surface early-warning signals, highlight hidden patterns across portfolios, and support risk and product teams with explainable insights that fit into existing decision flows.

Learn more→

Campaigns & Narrative Strategy

For political and social organizations, our agents map how narratives spread, test which messages resonate by region and demographic, and continuously recommend content and budget tweaks — turning noisy social data into sharper digital strategy.

Learn more→

Public Health & Public Systems

In the COVID-19 era, we worked on models that forecast growth vectors and cluster risks. Today, we bring that experience to public systems where uncertainty, limited data, and real-world constraints demand careful, transparent AI support.

Learn more→

Autonomous Finance & Trading

With projects like AgentVault, we build agents that consume market signals, execute strategies programmatically, and record every decision for review. This enables more adaptive, transparent approaches to on-chain and digital-asset markets.

Learn more→

Research & Knowledge Work

Through initiatives like HEO (Hypothesis-to-Experiment Orchestrator), our agents help researchers move from questions to designed experiments faster — combining literature analysis, protocol design, and verifiable experiment logging.

Learn more→

Compliance & Risk Management

Our agents continuously monitor regulatory changes, assess compliance gaps, and automate risk reporting. They help legal and compliance teams stay ahead of evolving requirements with real-time alerts and audit-ready documentation.
