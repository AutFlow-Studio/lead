HOW IT WORKS

From Freight Invoices To Reviewable Findings.

The system is configured around your operation, then used to process freight invoices against the billing information that matters to your business. It is designed to surface potential discrepancies so a person can review the findings before anything is treated as confirmed.

01
INGEST YOUR INVOICES
Bring the freight invoices your operation already receives into the configured audit workflow.
02
EXTRACT THE BILLING DATA
Relevant invoice information is structured so it can be checked consistently across large volumes.
03
CHECK AGAINST YOUR RULES
Compare rates, contracts, accessorials, duplicates, quantities, fuel charges, and other agreed billing rules.
04
FLAG POTENTIAL DISCREPANCIES
Items that warrant attention are placed into a review queue rather than automatically treated as confirmed errors.
05
HUMAN VERIFICATION
A person reviews the relevant evidence before findings are included in a final report.
06
STRUCTURED REPORTING
Verified findings are organized into a report your team can use to investigate and resolve billing issues.

WHAT GETS CHECKED

Configured Checks. Relevant Evidence.

The audit system can be configured to compare invoice information against the commercial and operational references that actually govern your freight billing.

↔
CONTRACT / RATE MATCH
Compare billed amounts against agreed carrier contracts, rate tables, lanes, or other configured references.
⊕
DUPLICATE CHARGES
Identify invoice records or charges that appear to require duplicate-billing review.
+
ACCESSORIAL CHARGES
Review accessorial charges against the rules and supporting information defined for the operation.
%
FUEL CHARGES
Check fuel-related billing against configured expectations and available reference information.
≡
WEIGHT / QUANTITY
Compare quantities, weights, and related invoice fields where those checks are relevant to the audit.
✓
BILLING RULES
Apply additional agreed rules that help identify invoice items requiring human attention.
HUMAN VERIFICATION

Software Finds Exceptions. People Verify Findings.

The system is designed to identify items that deserve attention. That does not make every flagged item a confirmed billing issue. Relevant evidence is reviewed before findings are reported.

✓Potential discrepancy is identified
✓Relevant invoice and reference data is reviewed
✓Finding is assessed by a person
✓Verified findings are separated from unconfirmed items
✓Structured report is prepared for resolution
DATA HANDLING

Your Data Stays Under Your Control.

Freight invoices and rate information can contain sensitive commercial data. The audit environment can be configured to run locally on your own machine or within your controlled environment, so working files can remain with you rather than being uploaded to a public SaaS dashboard.

▣
LOCAL AUDIT ENVIRONMENT
Designed around a customer-controlled environment instead of a shared online dashboard.
◧
LESS UNNECESSARY EXPOSURE
The goal is to avoid needlessly exposing freight invoices, carrier billing information, contracts, rate sheets, shipping data, and financial information.
✎
AGREED EXPECTATIONS
Data-handling and confidentiality expectations are discussed and defined before implementation begins. No system can promise zero risk; the aim is a clearly controlled setup.
