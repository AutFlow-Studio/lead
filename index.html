Why it matters

A launch is an explanation problem.

Most products are explained by the people who know them best, which is why the story often loses a stranger. A launch works better when the message is sharp, the product is shown working, and everything looks like it came from one place.

Easier to understand

We refine the headline, description and core message so the point lands without a call to explain it.

Shown, not described

Motion videos demonstrate how the product works, from different angles in Launch Pro.

One visual identity

Videos, thumbnails, share images and posts share a look, so the launch reads as one piece of work.

Assets you can reuse

Cuts and posts you can use for the announcement, for social distribution and for later updates.
