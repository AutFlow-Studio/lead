FAQ

Questions,
Answered.

What is included in the Complete Startup Launch?

A functional MVP, a professional website, brand identity, marketing videos, advertising and social media creatives, and launch preparation, delivered as one package for $4,997.

Is the MVP a fully functional product?

Yes. The MVP is a real, working product with core features and essential integrations, tested before delivery, not a prototype or static design. It is scoped as a minimum viable product, not a full-scale platform.

Can you build a product based on my idea?

That's exactly what the Startup Launch is for. We start by understanding your idea, then define what the MVP should include before we build.

What happens if my project requires a larger scope?

If the work goes substantially beyond the package, we'll tell you upfront and discuss the additional scope separately. We won't silently promise unlimited work at the package price.

What types of business problems can you solve?

Operational and business problems that cost you time, money, or opportunities: repetitive manual processes, bottlenecks, inefficient workflows, expensive admin tasks, poor information management, slow customer response, data processing, and disconnected tools. We work across industries, and we begin by studying your actual problem.

Do you build custom AI automation systems?

Yes, where AI is the right fit. Business Solutions can include AI-powered functionality and custom automation workflows, designed around your specific problem.

Can you integrate with our existing software?

We plan integrations with your existing tools as part of the solution architecture. Feasibility depends on the tools involved and the access they allow, and we'll confirm it while planning.

How does the project process work?

Every project follows four steps: understand your idea or problem, plan the scope and solution, build the product or system, then test, deploy, and hand over the finished work.

How is pricing determined?

We offer two packages: the Complete Startup Launch at $4,997 and Business Solutions at $1,997. If your project needs significantly more than a package covers, we'll discuss that scope with you separately.
