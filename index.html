THE PROBLEM

The Problem Isn't That
Your Team Isn't Checking.
It's That There's Too Much To Check.

Companies with real freight spend often work with multiple carriers, negotiated rates, accessorial charges, and fuel surcharges — spread across hundreds or thousands of invoices a month, in different formats from different systems. Reviewing every line item by hand is possible in theory. In practice, volume outpaces attention, and patterns across invoices get missed.

THE APPROACH

We Don't Replace Your Team. We Help It Focus.

Manual review doesn't usually fail because people aren't careful. It fails because volume outpaces attention. Instead of reviewing every invoice with the same depth, our workflow narrows a large invoice set down to the specific charges that actually deserve a closer look.

THE PROCESS

How The Audit Works.

You don't need to know what should be automated or what technology to use. You send us your invoices — we handle the rest, and check with you at each stage.

01

WHAT WE CHECK

Six Categories Of Potential Billing Issues.

Every flagged item is labeled as a potential discrepancy that requires verification — not treated as a confirmed error. We'd rather under-claim and be right than over-claim and lose your trust.

⧉
DUPLICATE BILLING
Repeated invoice, shipment, or PRO/tracking patterns that may indicate the same charge billed more than once.
%
RATE DISCREPANCIES
Billed amounts compared against agreed or contracted rates, where contract or rate sheet data is available.
+
ACCESSORIAL CHARGES
Additional charges — detention, liftgate, residential, reclassification — that look unexpected or unsupported.
⛽
FUEL & SURCHARGE ISSUES
Surcharge calculations that appear inconsistent with the carrier's published or contracted methodology.
⚖
WEIGHT & SHIPMENT DATA
Mismatches between shipment details on record and what was actually billed.
◈
OTHER BILLING ANOMALIES
Patterns that don't fit a standard category but are unusual enough to warrant a closer look.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need to change my existing software?
No. The initial audit works from exported invoice files — PDF, CSV, or Excel. No system replacement or integration is required to get started.
Do you need our contracts or rate sheets?
They're useful for rate validation, but the initial sample can still identify certain discrepancy categories — like duplicate billing or accessorial issues — without them.
Is AI making the decisions?
No. AI may assist with extracting and structuring messy documents. Core checks run on deterministic rules, followed by human verification, before anything is reported as a finding.
Can you guarantee savings?
No. We don't promise a savings number before we've reviewed your actual data. Some invoice sets have significant issues; some are clean. We won't know until we look.
What if you find nothing?
We tell you honestly. A clean sample is useful information too — it means your current process is working for that carrier.
How long does the audit take?
Typically 5–10 business days for the full engagement after we receive the required data. The free sample check is usually faster.
How much does it cost?
Typical full engagements run $5,000–$10,000, depending on carriers, invoice volume, audit period, and complexity. The initial invoice check is free.
What do I need to send for the free sample?
Ideally 30–90 days of invoices from one carrier, in PDF, CSV, or Excel format. A contract or rate sheet helps but isn't required.
Will this replace our Finance, AP, or Logistics team?
No. It's designed to help your existing team focus on the exceptions that deserve attention, instead of manually reviewing every invoice.
Is my data confidential?
We can sign an NDA before receiving sensitive documents, and only the people working on your audit have access to your files. Ask us for our current data-handling policy in writing.
