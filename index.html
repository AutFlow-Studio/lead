THE PROBLEM

Freight Billing Discrepancies
Can Stay Hidden.

Manual review can't cover every freight invoice. A single billing error may be small. Repeated across hundreds of invoices, thousands of shipments, multiple carriers, and multiple facilities, small discrepancies add up to meaningful financial leakage. The problem is rarely one huge mistake. It is small errors, repeated at scale.

WHAT WE SOLVE

The Discrepancies We Look For.

Carrier invoices can contain many kinds of billing issues. These are the categories the system is configured to check against your contracts and billing rules. Each flag is a potential discrepancy until it has been verified.

↻
RATE MISMATCHES
Charges that differ from negotiated contract rates or rate sheets.
⇄
DUPLICATE CHARGES
The same invoice or shipment reference billed more than once.
⌁
ACCESSORIAL CHARGES
Unexpected or unauthorized accessorial charges added to an invoice.
◷
FUEL CHARGES
Fuel charges that do not match the agreed schedule or contract terms.
◈
WEIGHT & QUANTITY
Billed weights or quantities that are inconsistent with shipment records.
⎘
OTHER BILLING ANOMALIES
Other irregularities measured against contracts and billing rules.

THE AUDIT PROCESS

How It Works.

Invoices → automated checks → flagged exceptions → human verification → report.

01
COLLECT & CONFIGURE
You provide freight invoices from your carriers, plus contracts, rate sheets, shipment records, and billing information where available. The system is configured around your carriers, rates, and billing rules.
02
EXTRACT & CHECK
The system extracts and structures invoice data, then checks rates, duplicates, accessorials, weights, and quantities.
03
VERIFY
Potential discrepancies are organized, then reviewed by a person before being treated as confirmed findings.
04
REPORT
You receive the invoice, charge, difference, reason for the flag, supporting information, and potential financial impact.
