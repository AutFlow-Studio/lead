FAQ

A few things worth knowing upfront.

What do I need to provide?+
Property details, access or existing footage, and a sense of what makes the property distinctive. We’ll guide you through exactly what’s needed once you get in touch.
What exactly is included?+
One cinematic property video, three social reels, three marketing covers with captions, one 15-second ad creative, and three rounds of revisions — all in the $500 package.
How long does production take?+
Timelines depend on the property and current queue. You’ll get a clear delivery estimate as soon as we scope the project with you.
Can I use the content for paid advertising?+
Yes — the package includes a dedicated 15-second cut built specifically for paid ad placements, alongside the organic content.
What formats do I receive?+
The main film in 16:9 and 9:16, reels and ad creative in 9:16, and thumbnails sized for social and listing use.
How do revisions work?+
Three rounds are included with every package. You’ll review the cut and share notes; we refine it until it represents the property the way you want.
Can you work with existing property photos or videos?+
Yes — if you already have quality photo or video material, we can build the package from it. Let us know what you have when you reach out.
